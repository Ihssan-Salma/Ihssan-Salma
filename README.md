## `03` · Selected projects

### 🤖 Multi-Agent Reservation Replacement Platform

An **AI-powered multi-agent platform** for businesses such as restaurants, hotels, and beauty salons. It detects last-minute reservation cancellations, verifies booking details, identifies suitable replacements, contacts candidates, confirms the new booking, and updates the calendar.

**Key challenges:** concurrent replacement workflows, multi-business support, and secure tenant data isolation.

`Multi-Agent Systems` · `AI Automation` · `Reservation Workflows` · `Data Isolation`

### 📝 [eGrading — Academic Evaluation Platform](https://github.com/eayzaid/eGrading)

A web platform developed in a **two-person team** under the supervision of the head of the Mathematics and Computer Science department at ENSAM Meknès. Built with **Spring Boot, React, and MySQL** to digitize exam grading; **deployed in production and used for official academic exams**.

`Spring Boot` · `React` · `MySQL` · `Production Deployment`
